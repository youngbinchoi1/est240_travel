AI Video
Julie Yim
Canva AI

This video presents a short narrative centered on the contrast between careful creation and sudden destruction. This piece demonstrates key mise-en-scène elements, including intentional use of lighting (soft vs. harsh), color (warm vs. cold), and composition (stable vs. chaotic) to reinforce the emotional shift. It also incorporates montage techniques such as match cuts and rapid editing to create continuity and build tension. Overall, the video functions as a visual argument about the fragility of creation and how quickly something meaningful can be lost.

AI Video
Juhyun Bae
Kling AI

This video opens on a serene lake at golden sunrise, where a small crowd gathers to admire a white swan gliding gracefully across the calm water. As the camera follows the swan, it slowly descends below the waterline, and the mood flips. Beneath the peaceful surface, the swan's webbed feet are paddling frantically, churning up bubbles and silt in a desperate effort to keep moving. The serene image we admired from above was hiding a struggle below. The film delivers its message entirely through visuals: grace on the surface is often powered by an unseen fight underneath.

hook-and-twist structure is used to show how music changes an ordinary school moment into an imaginative journey through motorcycle history. It begins with a student peacefully listening to music on the school grass, then a colorful water tsunami transforms the scene into a racing sequence where the motorcycle, rider outfit, and background change across different eras. AI Video
Dae Hwan Kim
Kling AI

An empty vintage Japanese local train moves through a snowy landscape. Two young men in winter coats stand by the fare box at the front of the car. The video opens in a dim, moody interior with murky green-gray shadows, then cuts to tight profile close-ups of the two men talking. It ends by pulling back to the same wide aisle view, now flooded with bright daylight as the windows reveal an endless snow-covered landscape outside. The mood shifts from dark and enclosed to luminous and open.

AI Video
Juyi Bae
Meta AI

A massive meteor dramatically descends toward Earth, creating a sense of imminent danger. As tension peaks, the meteor reveals itself as a chocolate chip cookie falling into milk. There is a transition from apocalyptic dread to playful, ordinary reality. To emphasize this contrast, prompts were engineered using dramatic lighting, slow motion, and match cuts. This short video is created to deliver the message, that shifting our perspective can transform an ordinary moment into a surprising cinematic experience.

Youngbin Choi
Canva AI

A boy is frustrated, hesitating to dive into the pool. As he dives in with bravery, the pool turns into a wonderful galaxy. There is a transition from fear & realism to awe & fantasy. To emphasize this contrast, prompt was designed to make two shots directly contrast in their style. This short montage is created to deliver the message, that attempting scary and difficult tasks with courage can bring us thrilling experiences.
