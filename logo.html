About the Logo

Identity Design

The Travel Group mark combines a rising airplane silhouette with a complete encircling ring, broken at the base by two layered motion lines, and anchored by an italic serif wordmark that names the subject outright. The composition is symmetrical, the airplane is centred, and the wordmark sits on the lower axis so the eye returns to it after tracing the flight path.

The entire mark is rendered in a metallic gold finish on a near-black field, with a flat black-and-white variant for print and a flat single-tone variant for icon use. The mark belongs to a family. It is designed to function alongside each member's personal mark, sharing an amber accent and a dark background while keeping a distinct form. The group's mark says where we are looking; each member's mark says who is looking.

Reading the Mark

Four Points

01 · Denotation

Airplane & Circle

An aircraft mid-flight, a globe-line it crosses, the trail of its passage, and a wordmark that names the subject. The denotation is immediate and unambiguous: travel.

02 · Connotation

Gold on Black

The gold-on-black palette connotes warmth, distance, and golden-hour light. Against a near-black field the gold reads as luminous and isolated, a single point of light on a dark map. It draws on the visual grammar of premium hospitality and vintage cartography.

03 · Scalability

Three-Tier Reduction

The full metallic finish carries the large size. A flat black-on-white print version preserves the silhouette without colour, satisfying the brief's monochrome requirement. A flat single-tone gold variant takes over at favicon and nav-badge size, where gradients and thin strokes would otherwise collapse.

04 · Shared Identity

One Family, Seven Marks

The group logo is designed to function as a family with each member's personal mark. The shared amber accent and dark background establish visual continuity across pages, while the distinct forms encode the collective-versus-individual dynamic at the heart of the project.

AI Process

Prompts and Tool Used

Per the Module 8 requirement to show the prompts and the AI used. The mark was developed through the prompt set below, then refined until the airplane was centred and the wordmark held its baseline at every size.

Tool · Image generator (Midjourney / DALL·E class)
Prompt 1 · Full Mark

A circular travel emblem on a deep black background. A gold airplane silhouette ascends diagonally from lower-left to upper-right and breaks through the top of an outlined gold circle. Below the airplane, two layered motion swooshes curve like vapor trails. The word "travel" is centred across the lower half in a bold italic serif, in the same gold. Flat vector logo style, metallic gold finish on pure black, perfectly symmetrical composition.

Prompt 2 · Monochrome Variant

The same emblem rendered for print: a pure black silhouette on white. Circle, ascending airplane breaking the top, layered motion swooshes, and the italic serif wordmark "travel" centred below. All in a single flat tone, no gradient, no shadow, scalable.

Prompt 3 · Icon Variant

Favicon-scale version. The same full mark, flat gold on black, no gradient, generous padding on all sides so the airplane is not clipped when reduced. Symmetrical.
