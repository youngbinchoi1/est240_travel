Group Logo · Module 8

Identity by
flight.

A breakdown of the visual choices behind the Travel Group mark. An airplane in flight inside a complete circle, a brushed gold finish on a black field, and a wordmark anchored beneath. Read denotatively as travel, connotatively as passage, light, and the analogue romance of the journey.

Three sizes

Per the Module 8 brief: three sizes, with the middle size in black and white. All three descend from the same drawing so the mark stays recognisable from header to favicon.

LARGE · COLOR
Metallic gold, full mark
MEDIUM · B/W
Print & monochrome
SMALL · ICON
Favicon & nav badge
01 · DENOTATION

Airplane & Circle

The primary mark combines a rising airplane silhouette with a complete encircling ring, broken at the base by two layered motion lines. At the denotative level this reads immediately and without ambiguity as travel: an aircraft mid-flight, the globe-line it crosses, the trail of its passage, and a wordmark that names the subject outright. The composition is symmetrical, the airplane is centred, and the wordmark sits on the lower axis so the eye returns to it after tracing the flight path.

02 · CONNOTATION

Gold on Black

The gold-on-black palette connotes warmth, distance, and the quality of golden-hour light that defines travel photography. Against a near-black field the gold reads as luminous and isolated, a single point of light on a dark map. It draws on the visual grammar of premium hospitality, vintage cartography, and analogue film aesthetics, framing the journey as something curated rather than merely documented.

An anchor does not float free of meaning; the design fixes it. — after Stuart Hall
03 · SCALABILITY

Three-Tier Reduction

The mark is built to survive scale. The full metallic finish carries the large size where detail and gradient can breathe. A flat black-on-white print version preserves the same silhouette without colour, which is the test the brief requires. A flat single-tone gold variant takes over at favicon and nav-badge size, where gradients and thin strokes would otherwise collapse into mud. Each version is the same drawing, just calibrated to its job.

04 · SHARED IDENTITY

One family, seven marks

The group logo is designed to function as a family with each member's personal mark. A shared amber accent and dark background establish visual continuity across the team's individual sites, while the distinct forms — an airplane for the group, individual typographic and heraldic marks for members — encode the collective-versus-individual dynamic at the heart of the project. The group's mark says where we are looking; each member's mark says who is looking.

The AI prompts

Per the Module 8 requirement to show the prompts and the AI used. The mark was developed through the prompt set below, then refined until the airplane was centred and the wordmark held its baseline at every size.

Tool: image generator (Midjourney / DALL·E class).

Prompt 1 · Full mark. A circular travel emblem on a deep black background. A gold airplane silhouette ascends diagonally from lower-left to upper-right and breaks through the top of an outlined gold circle. Below the airplane, two layered motion swooshes curve like vapor trails. The word "travel" is centred across the lower half in a bold italic serif, in the same gold. Flat vector logo style, metallic gold finish on pure black, perfectly symmetrical composition.

Prompt 2 · Monochrome variant. The same emblem rendered for print: a pure black silhouette on white. Circle, ascending airplane breaking the top, layered motion swooshes, and the italic serif wordmark "travel" centred below. All in a single flat tone, no gradient, no shadow, scalable.

Prompt 3 · Icon variant. Favicon-scale version. The same full mark, flat gold on black, no gradient, generous padding on all sides so the airplane is not clipped when reduced. Symmetrical.
