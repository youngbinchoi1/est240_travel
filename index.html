About the Platform

Travel Genre in YouTube Platform

In today's digital media landscape, the travel genre on YouTube has evolved beyond simple sightseeing guides into a distinct documentary form of storytelling, which offers viewers deep immersion and a powerful sense of vicarious, emotional fulfillment. Contemporary travel creators fluidly intercut dynamic first-person camera work with sweeping, god's-eye views of the natural world, intensely stirring the public's desire to escape the routines of everyday life.

This group page was conceived to examine, from multiple angles, how this highly sophisticated form of digital travel content inherits and reinterprets the audiovisual grammar of traditional film and video media. Drawing on in-depth critiques of travel channels with distinct personalities and strategies, we explore how the staging of on-screen space, the rhythm of editing, and the narrative anchoring techniques that establish a host's credibility work together to build robust parasocial relationships with viewers. Furthermore, our group substantially practices this exploration by creating an AI Mise-en-scène & Montage video, which creates a rhetorical meaning through a visual hook and connotational twist. .
